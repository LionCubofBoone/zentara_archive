[[PC's/Kieran\|Kieran]]'s wife
Co-owner and head cook at [[The World/Nyssara Kingdom of Veils and Incense/Dorn's Hollow/The Emerald Taigh ('tie')\|The Emerald Taigh ('tie')]]
Pronounced May-eve 

Amongst the other Firbolg children, Kieran was closest to Maebh, a girl around his age. Through their teens and into adulthood they remained inseparable. Mae bolstered Kieran with her courage, and in turn, he tempered her impulsivity with his second guessing.
Mae was a budding athlete and wanted to hone her skills. Keiran, a serious homebody who is rather codependant, wanted the taste of exploration without venturing out on his own, so he followed Mae. 

For some time, they travelled (where?) so Mae could train and learn from various schools of martial artists. They eventually fell in with an adventuring party for several years.

Frustrated by being the cook and ‘bag boy’ of the group, and terrified of every encounter in which he was utterly useless, Kieran decided to bow out of the group and return home to safety, to the life he knew. His only regret was leaving Mae, who had adapted brilliantly to the adventuring life and wanted to stay on to help the party accomplish their goal. They were the sort who had long since realized their love for one another was a deeper, more intangible thing, but had not had the time or care to mess with the equilibrium of their relationship. Despite this, Kieran vowed to wait for her return while he rebuilt his life amongst the clan. 

After a few more years, Maebh returned home and ignited their relationship. They were soon married. With neither of them religious, this was a private exchanging of vows, followed by a decently rowdy party.
