[[NPCs/Klelvar Family\|Klelvar Family]]
- missing, presumed dead. Mother avoids discussing his disappearance, other family members seem to have forgotten about him
- they still set a place for him at the table by habit, but if consciously mentioned they may be confused 
- Klelvars got their title/lands because of him. He held a high position as a scribe and ethics advisor in the Church, did a great duty and was awarded a name and land for his family who have become very rich
- the church passed his disappearance off as a noble soul martyred for the church and gave no details
