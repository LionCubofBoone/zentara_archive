Copying spells into the book: 
15g/spell level due to the scarcity of paper in this world-- entirely controlled by the Church so very hard to get in the first place. 

- per DM you can take notes on a single page and attempt to recreate in spell book later. This comes with a higher DC and chance of spell failing bc you have to kind of figure it out instead of just reading the scroll
