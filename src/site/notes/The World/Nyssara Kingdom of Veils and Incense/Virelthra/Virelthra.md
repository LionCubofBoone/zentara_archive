Virelthra is the holy capital of Nyssara. Headquarters for the Covenant of the Duskmaiden, Virelthra was one of the few settlements to survive the Titan's Dusk. Church doctrine dictates that this is because the Maiden chose this location to be the center of the New World, under her tutelage. Virelthra happened to be strategically, centrally located following the events of the Dusk, allowing its forces easy access to the entire country through well-planned and travelled routes.

Thought wardens: city guard/police, patrol on the hour every hour. Least busy time 6-8am

The Aureate Spire: "Where the Heavens touch the Throne The Aureate Spire serves as the theocratic seat of power for the entire country. It is the ecclesiastical and political epicenter of Nyssara. Oppressively radiant, the walls are inlaid with gold leaf that refracts light unnaturally, creating a constant daylight effect - even at night. Spiraled alabaster towers looms, connected by crystalline bridges and frescoed domes - depicting divine scenes, such as when the Duskmaiden slew the Titans and claimed protective rite over Zenaris.
