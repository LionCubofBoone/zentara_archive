Colors: Blue and Black
Nyssara, the westernmost continent of Zentara, is home to a population of approximately 3.7 million people of varrying species, race, and tribe. The capital of Nyssara, Virelthra, is the primary seat of power for the Covenant of the Duskmaiden. The capital city information is continued in its separate article.  
 To the West, the coast of Nyssara welcomes merchant vessles from Tavros. To the east, the coast boasts Nyssara's primary naval force as it looms closest to the central, natural zone of the two countries. To the northeast lies the spiritual academy that acts as the only school to teach magic throughout the country (legally)..
